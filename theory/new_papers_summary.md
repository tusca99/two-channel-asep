# New papers beyond Pronina & Kolomeisky (2007) — key messages for the talk

> Goal: convey without reading the papers — what they fix, what survives to $L\to\infty$.

## 1. Xiao *et al.*, Chin. Phys. B **19**, 090202 (2010)
**Title:** *Effect of unequal injection rates and different hopping rates on ASEP with junction — vertical cluster MF with local boundary correlations.*

**What they do:**
- Keep the *local* correlator $C = \langle n_1 m_L\rangle - \langle n_1\rangle\langle m_L\rangle$ instead of factorizing $\langle n_1 m_L\rangle\approx\langle n_1\rangle\langle m_L\rangle$ (our eq 2).
- 2-site (and 4-site) cluster at the narrow entrance → self-consistent $\alpha_{\rm eff}= \alpha f(\rho_{\rm opp},C)$ for $\rho_1,\rho_2,C$.
- Different $\alpha_{1,2}$ and hopping rates allowed.

**Key result:**
- $\beta$ boundaries shift toward our SIM (vs bulk MF) — closes ~30–50 % of the $0.23$ vs $0.33$ gap (our Fig. 27/31). HD/LD remains, LD/LD narrows.
- Lesson for our talk: *MFT failure is a boundary-correlation effect, not a bulk error.*

**One-liner for slide:** *2010 = first fix: keep $C$ at the entrance, $\beta$ boundary moves toward SIM.*

## 2. Tian *et al.*, Chin. Phys. B **26**, 020503 (2017)
**Title:** *Spurious symmetry-broken phase in a bidirectional two-lane ASEP with narrow entrances — $N$-cluster, exponential decay; one S-B phase vanishes as $L\to\infty$.*

**What they do:**
- $N$-cluster mean-field ($N=2\to6$) + Monte-Carlo finite-size scaling $L=100$–$2000+$ at proper $c\approx100$ ($cL^2$ budget, as we note).
- Track SSB order parameter $\mathrm{std}(\rho_1-\rho_2)$ vs $L$.

**Key result:**
- **One broken phase is spurious.** HD/LD survives (first-order, $C$ is essential), **LD/LD vanishes exponentially** $\sim \exp(-L/\xi)$ — the orange band in our Fig. 2/5 is the fragile one.
- Our Fig. 5 drift for $L\ge4000$ at $c=50$ is exactly the under-equilibration Tian warns about; at $c=100$ the LD/LD band collapses faster.
- Lesson for our talk: *Bands shrink — Fig. 5 grey shade is not physics. Thermodynamic limit likely has only HD/LD (if any) as true SSB.*

**One-liner for slide:** *2017 = $N$-cluster + scaling: LD/LD is spurious, HD/LD may survive — our narrowing (Fig. 29/30) is the finite-size signature.*

## How to say it in 60 s (outlook slide cue)

> *Bulk MF factorizes the entrance — keep the neighbour correlator $C$ (2010) and the $\beta$ boundary moves from 0.33 toward our SIM 0.23. But even then, $N$-cluster shows one broken band is finite-size: LD/LD decays exponentially (2017). So our job next is: 4-site $\alpha_{\rm eff}(C)$ + proper $c\approx100$ scaling to $L=8000$–$12000$ to see if anything remains at $L\to\infty$.*
